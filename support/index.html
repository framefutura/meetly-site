Support

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Most questions are answered below. If not, write to framefutura@gmail.com — we reply within two business days and offer near-priority support for Spectrum subscribers.

Getting started

  • Sign in: Meetly uses ‹Sign in with Apple› — there is no separate password to remember.
  • Add events: tap + on any day in the calendar. To bring in your Apple Calendar, grant calendar access when prompted; you can import individual calendars in settings.
  • Create a group: Groups are private and invite-only. Tap New Group, give it a name, then invite members by name or email. Invitations arrive through iCloud sharing.
  • Get recommendations: open the Discovery tab, pick a date range and level of interest — Meetly suggests activities from official event sources and local venues.

Sync and notifications

  • Changes not appearing on another device? Sign in with the same Apple ID, wait a few seconds, then pull to refresh. iCloud sync can briefly lag on shared groups.
  • No notifications? Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Meetly; also confirm your device is not in Focus mode. Notification permissions are requested only when you first use notification features.
  • Background refresh: Meetly uses Apple’s background refresh to keep calendars and reminders current; iOS decides the exact timing.

Subscriptions

  • Where to manage: everything (plans, renewal, cancellation) lives in App Store → Subscriptions on your iPhone/iPad. Meetly cannot cancel on your behalf.
  • Upgrading Prism ↔ Spectrum: switch plans in the App Store; you keep the equivalent remaining time per Apple rules.
  • Refunds: requested through Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com). We cannot process refunds directly.
  • Family Sharing (Spectrum): share a subscription with relatives who are in your Apple Family Sharing group.

Reporting content or members

  1. Long-press the member or the group Report.
  2. Pick a reason and (optionally) add details in the confirmation screen.
  3. Reports are confidential to the reporter’s identity, reviewed within 24 hours, and acted on when they violate our Terms.
  4. To stop seeing a group immediately, leave it (group screen → Leave Group).

Deleting your account

  1. Go to Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account.
  2. Confirm the dialog. On-device data and iCloud records are erased permanently.
  3. Cancel any active subscriptions separately in App Store → Subscriptions.
  4. Re-login after deletion starts with a fresh account.

See our Privacy Policy for what deletion covers and Apple’s deletion propagation.
